One-Bowl Banana Bread

A moist, no-mixer quick bread for overripe bananas.

By BreadScopeCraft Kitchen·Updated Nov 24, 2026·Jump to recipe ↓
Makes1 loaf (about 10 slices)
Prep10 min
Risenone
Bake55–65 min
Oven350°F (175°C)
WHY IT WORKS

Banana bread is a quick bread: it rises with baking soda instead of yeast, so there’s no waiting. Very ripe, heavily speckled bananas give the best flavor and moisture.

ADVERTISEMENT

Ingredients

  • 350 gvery ripe bananas, mashed
  • 75 gbutter, melted
  • 150 gsugar (white or brown)
  • 1large egg
  • 5 mlvanilla extract
  • 5 gbaking soda
  • 2 gfine salt
  • 190 gall-purpose flour

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a 9×5-inch (23×13 cm) loaf pan.
  2. In a bowl, mash the bananas. Stir in the melted butter, then the sugar, egg and vanilla.
  3. Sprinkle the baking soda and salt over the mixture and stir in. Add the flour and fold just until no dry streaks remain.
  4. Pour into the pan and bake 55–65 minutes, until a skewer in the center comes out clean.
  5. Cool 10 minutes in the pan, then turn out onto a rack.
BAKER’S TIP

Overmixing makes quick breads tough. Stop stirring as soon as the flour disappears.

Frequently asked questions

How ripe should the bananas be?

The riper the better — mostly brown peels are ideal.

Can I add nuts or chocolate?

Yes. Fold in up to 1 cup (about 120 g) at the end.

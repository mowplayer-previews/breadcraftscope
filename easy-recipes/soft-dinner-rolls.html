Soft Dinner Rolls

Pillowy pull-apart rolls for any table.

By BreadCraftScope Kitchen·Updated Nov 24, 2026·Jump to recipe ↓
Makes12 rolls
Prep25 min
Rise1 ½–2 hours
Bake18–22 min
Oven375°F (190°C)
WHY IT WORKS

An enriched dough with milk, butter, sugar and an egg gives these rolls a tender, fluffy crumb. They bake close together in one pan so the sides stay soft.

ADVERTISEMENT

Ingredients

  • 480 gall-purpose flour
  • 7 ginstant yeast
  • 50 gsugar
  • 8 gfine salt
  • 240 gwarm milk
  • 1large egg
  • 60 gbutter, softened, plus more for brushing

Method

  1. Combine the flour, yeast, sugar and salt. Add the warm milk, egg and butter and mix into a soft dough.
  2. Knead 8–10 minutes, until smooth and slightly tacky. Cover and let rise about 1 hour, until doubled.
  3. Divide into 12 equal pieces (about 70 g each). Roll each into a tight ball and arrange in a greased 9×13-inch pan.
  4. Cover and let rise 30–45 minutes, until puffy and touching. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  5. Bake 18–22 minutes, until golden. Brush with melted butter while warm.
BAKER’S TIP

Use a kitchen scale to divide the dough. Even pieces bake evenly.

Frequently asked questions

Can I make them ahead?

Shape the rolls, cover and refrigerate overnight. Let them sit at room temperature about 1 hour before baking.

Can I freeze them?

Yes. Freeze baked, cooled rolls in a bag for up to 3 months and warm them in the oven.

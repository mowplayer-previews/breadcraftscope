Easy No-Knead Bread

Four ingredients, five minutes of work and a crackly bakery-style crust.

By BreadScopeCraft Kitchen·Updated Nov 24, 2026·Jump to recipe ↓
Makes1 round loaf (about 10 slices)
Prep10 min
Rise12–18 hours + 1–2 hours
Bake45–50 min
Oven450°F (230°C)
WHY IT WORKS

Mix flour, water, salt and a tiny amount of yeast, leave it overnight, then bake it in a very hot covered pot. Long fermentation does the work of kneading and builds flavor.

ADVERTISEMENT

Ingredients

  • 430 gbread flour (or all-purpose)
  • 8 gfine salt
  • 1 ginstant yeast
  • 345 gwater, room temperature

Method

  1. In a large bowl, stir together the flour, salt and yeast. Add the water and mix until no dry flour remains. The dough will be shaggy and sticky.
  2. Cover and leave at room temperature for 12–18 hours, until the surface is bubbly and the dough has more than doubled.
  3. Turn the dough onto a floured counter. Fold the edges into the center to form a round, then flip it seam-side down onto parchment paper.
  4. Cover loosely and let rise 1–2 hours, until puffy. About 45 minutes before baking, put a lidded Dutch oven in the oven and preheat to 450°F (230°C).
  5. Lift the dough by the parchment into the hot pot. Cover and bake 30 minutes, then uncover and bake 15–20 minutes more, until deep brown.
  6. Cool on a rack for at least 1 hour before slicing.
BAKER’S TIP

No Dutch oven? Bake on a preheated baking sheet with a metal pan of hot water on the rack below for the first 20 minutes.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use active dry yeast?

Yes. Use a slightly heaping ¼ teaspoon; there’s no need to dissolve it first for such a long rise.

Can I add whole wheat flour?

Replace up to 100 g of the flour with whole wheat and add 1–2 tablespoons more water.

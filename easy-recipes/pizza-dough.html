Easy Homemade Pizza Dough

Stretchy, flavorful dough for crisp home-oven pizza.

By BreadCraftScope Kitchen·Updated Nov 24, 2026·Jump to recipe ↓
Makes4 dough balls (four 10–12 inch pizzas)
Prep15 min
Rise2 hours, or 1–3 days in the fridge
Bake8–12 min per pizza
Ovenas hot as your oven goes, 500–550°F (260–290°C)
WHY IT WORKS

A simple dough of flour, water, salt, yeast and a splash of olive oil. A slow rise — ideally a day in the fridge — makes it easier to stretch and much tastier.

ADVERTISEMENT

Ingredients

  • 500 gbread flour or “00” flour
  • 325 gwater
  • 10 gfine salt
  • 3 ginstant yeast
  • 15 golive oil

Method

  1. Mix the flour and yeast, add the water and salt, and mix until combined. Add the olive oil and knead 8–10 minutes until smooth.
  2. Cover and let rise 1 hour at room temperature. Divide into 4 pieces and shape each into a tight ball.
  3. Place the balls in oiled containers. Either let them rise 1 hour more, or refrigerate for 1–3 days (take them out 2 hours before baking).
  4. Preheat the oven with a pizza stone or steel (or an upturned baking sheet) for 45 minutes at its highest setting.
  5. Stretch a dough ball by hand to 10–12 inches, top lightly and bake 8–12 minutes, until the crust is blistered and the cheese bubbles.
BAKER’S TIP

Go light on toppings. A heavy load of sauce and cheese makes the center soggy in a home oven.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my dough keep springing back?

The gluten is tight. Cover it and let it rest 15 minutes, then try stretching again.

Can I freeze pizza dough?

Yes. Freeze oiled dough balls in bags for up to 3 months and thaw overnight in the fridge.

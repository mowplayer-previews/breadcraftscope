Crispy Sourdough Discard Crackers

Turn leftover starter into a crunchy snack.

By BreadCraftScope Kitchen·Updated Nov 24, 2026·Jump to recipe ↓
Makesabout 40 crackers
Prep15 min
Risenone (rest 30 min)
Bake20–25 min
Oven350°F (175°C)
WHY IT WORKS

Discard is the starter you remove before feeding. Mixed with flour, olive oil and salt, then rolled thin, it bakes into crisp, savory crackers — no rising needed.

ADVERTISEMENT

Ingredients

  • 200 gsourdough discard
  • 60 gall-purpose or whole wheat flour
  • 30 golive oil
  • 3 gfine salt
  • seeds, herbs and flaky salt, for topping

Method

  1. Mix the discard, flour, olive oil and salt into a soft dough. Cover and rest 30 minutes.
  2. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Roll the dough very thin — about 1/16 inch (1–2 mm) — on parchment.
  3. Brush lightly with water, sprinkle with seeds and flaky salt, and cut into squares with a pizza cutter. Prick with a fork.
  4. Slide the parchment onto a baking sheet and bake 20–25 minutes, until golden and crisp. Thinner edge pieces may be ready sooner.
  5. Cool completely, then break apart along the cuts.
BAKER’S TIP

The thinner you roll, the crispier the crackers. Uneven thickness is the most common reason some stay chewy.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use discard straight from the fridge?

Yes. Discard keeps in the fridge for about a week.

How do I store them?

In an airtight container for up to 1 week.

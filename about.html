About BreadCraftScope

BreadCraftScope helps home bakers make great bread with fewer steps and less guesswork. We publish beginner-friendly recipes and plain-English guides to how bread actually works.

Every recipe lists ingredients by weight and by volume, gives realistic timings and explains what to look for at each stage, because dough doesn’t always follow the clock.

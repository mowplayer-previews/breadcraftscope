How baker’s percentages work

Flour is always 100%. Every other ingredient is a percentage of the flour weight. A dough with 500 g flour and 350 g water is 70% hydration.

Typical ranges

  • Sandwich bread: 60–65% hydration
  • Rustic and no-knead loaves: 70–78%
  • Focaccia: 75–85%
  • Pizza: 60–70%
  • Salt: about 1.8–2.2%
  • Instant yeast: 0.2% for long, slow rises up to about 1.5% for same-day breads
  • Sourdough starter: commonly 10–25%

Note: if you use a lot of sourdough starter, remember it also contains flour and water, so the true hydration is slightly different.

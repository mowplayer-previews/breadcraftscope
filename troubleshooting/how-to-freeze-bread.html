How to Freeze and Thaw Bread

Lock in freshness for weeks and bring it back to life.

By BreadCraftScope Kitchen·Updated Nov 24, 2026·5 min read
THE SHORT ANSWER

Let bread cool completely, slice it if you’ll use it a piece at a time, wrap it tightly and freeze for up to about 3 months. Toast slices from frozen or thaw a whole loaf at room temperature and refresh it in the oven.

In this guide
  1. 01  Freezing
  2. 02  Thawing
  3. 03  What freezes well
STEP / 01

Freezing

Wrap cooled bread tightly in plastic wrap or a freezer bag, pressing out as much air as you can. A second layer of foil helps prevent freezer burn.

Label the bag with the date.

ADVERTISEMENT
STEP / 02

Thawing

Toast single slices straight from the freezer.

Thaw a whole loaf in its wrapping at room temperature for 2–3 hours, then warm it at 350°F (175°C) for 10 minutes to refresh the crust.

STEP / 03

What freezes well

Almost all breads freeze well, including sourdough, sandwich loaves, rolls and banana bread.

Pizza dough and shaped rolls can also be frozen raw.

BAKER’S TIP

Freeze half of a fresh loaf on baking day. It will taste far fresher than bread left out for four days.

Frequently asked questions

Can I refreeze thawed bread?

You can, but the texture suffers. Freeze in portions you’ll use.

Why did my frozen bread get ice crystals?

Air got in. Wrap more tightly and use a freezer bag.
